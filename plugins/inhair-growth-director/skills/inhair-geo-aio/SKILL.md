# INHAIR GEO / AIO

Improve the probability that AI systems can correctly understand, retrieve, summarize and cite INHAIR content. Do not claim guaranteed inclusion in an AI answer.

## Evaluate
- Is the entity/brand/product/category identity unambiguous?
- Are important facts stated directly and verifiably?
- Does the page answer real user questions in concise passages?
- Are claims supported by visible evidence or authoritative source material?
- Is authorship/expertise/provenance clear where appropriate?
- Can a model distinguish product facts from marketing language?
- Are comparisons framed with explicit criteria rather than vague superiority claims?
- Is the page internally linked from semantically relevant hubs?
- Do relevant competitors answer an important user question more clearly or comprehensively, and is that gap supported by current web evidence?

## Competitive benchmark
When useful, route through Competitor Intelligence and apply `../../references/inhair-competitor-policy.md`.
- NádhernéVlasy.cz: primary general benchmark.
- ItalyStyle.cz: first benchmark for Framesi.
- NaVlas.cz: secondary professional-hair benchmark.

Do not copy competitor text. Convert observable answerability/entity/content gaps into original INHAIR blocks, FAQs, tables or page architecture.

## Improve
Favor:
- answer-first passages,
- concise definitions,
- structured comparison criteria,
- explicit product suitability and limitations,
- factual tables where useful,
- clear dates/version context for time-sensitive content,
- source attribution when external factual claims need it,
- consistent entity naming.

GEO/AIO is not “write for robots”. Human usefulness, factual precision and sourceability are the foundation.

## Measurement
Use observable proxies rather than invented AIO scores:
- AI referral traffic in analytics,
- branded/non-branded query growth,
- citations/mentions found through current web checks when accessible,
- changes in landing-page engagement/conversion,
- indexation and search visibility,
- closure of validated competitor answerability gaps.
