# INHAIR Paid Growth

Use paid media as both a performance channel and a source of commercial-intent evidence for SEO and content decisions.

## Google Ads
Analyze as relevant:
- spend, revenue/conversion value, ROAS,
- campaign/ad-group/product/search-term performance,
- conversion rates and cost efficiency,
- query intent and landing-page match,
- wasted spend patterns,
- branded vs non-branded context,
- device/time/product segmentation where material.

## OpenAI Ads
Analyze as relevant:
- campaign/ad/ad-group delivery,
- impressions, clicks, spend, conversions and conversion quality,
- creative/landing-page alignment,
- audience/context signals exposed by the platform,
- trend and period comparisons.

## Cross-channel use
Feed validated commercial insights back to SEO:
- converting paid queries can reveal organic content/landing opportunities,
- organic pages with strong conversion can inform paid landing strategy,
- weak paid + weak organic may indicate an offer/intent mismatch rather than a channel-only problem.

## Guardrail
Do not modify bids, budgets, status, targeting, campaigns, ad groups, ads or conversion settings without explicit approval of the exact proposed change.
