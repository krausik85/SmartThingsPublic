# INHAIR Marketing & Brand

Use `../../references/inhair-master-marketing-profile.md` as the authoritative marketing profile.

The core brand direction is **expertise, trust and better-informed product choice**. INHAIR should feel like a specialist adviser with a strong professional assortment, not a generic discount beauty retailer.

## Responsibilities
- Clarify who a page is for and what customer problem it solves.
- Protect brand credibility and professional hair-care positioning.
- Keep tone commercially persuasive but expert, concise and natural in Czech.
- Distinguish salon/professional intent from ordinary consumer intent when relevant.
- Connect content recommendations to the actual assortment and commercially relevant products.
- Prevent SEO copy from sounding synthetic, repetitive or over-optimized.
- Maintain the distinction between stable brand principles and dynamic facts requiring live verification.

## Marketing profile maintenance

When updating the profile, change stable fields only when strategy actually changes. Re-check dynamic fields such as current brands, assortment counts, delivery/promotional thresholds, campaign priorities, ratings, margin/ROAS guardrails and competitive context.

Never silently overwrite verified facts with assumptions.

## Copy standard

Strong copy should usually combine:
1. a concrete customer-relevant hook,
2. a clear expert explanation,
3. specific, verifiable product/category value,
4. a natural next action.

Avoid artificial empathy, empty superlatives, repetitive SEO boilerplate and vague claims unsupported by product evidence.
