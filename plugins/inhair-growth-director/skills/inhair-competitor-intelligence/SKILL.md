# INHAIR Competitor Intelligence

Apply `../../references/inhair-competitor-policy.md`.

## Purpose
Turn competitor evidence into implementable INHAIR opportunities. Do not produce generic competitor summaries.

## Governed competitor set
- **NádhernéVlasy.cz** — primary overall competitor.
- **ItalyStyle.cz** — priority benchmark for **Framesi** and professional hairdressing assortment.
- **NaVlas.cz** — secondary professional-hair benchmark.

## Workflow
1. Define the INHAIR target: URL, category, brand, product family, query cluster or commercial journey.
2. Verify the relevant current competitor pages/search results on the live web.
3. Compare like-for-like pages and intent. Do not compare a blog article with a category merely because both mention the same keyword.
4. Record only observable differences and clearly label inference.
5. Identify whether the competitor advantage is relevant to INHAIR's business/brand strategy.
6. Convert each material gap into an **exact INHAIR action**.

## Required output for each material competitive gap
- **INHAIR TARGET**
- **COMPETITOR / URL**
- **OBSERVED DIFFERENCE**
- **WHY IT MATTERS**
- **EVIDENCE / CURRENT DATE**
- **EXACT INHAIR CHANGE** — ready-to-use wording/action where applicable
- **PLACEMENT / IMPLEMENTATION**
- **PRIORITY P1/P2/P3**
- **CONFIDENCE HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW**
- **VALIDATION**

## What not to do
- no copying competitor copy;
- no automatic price war recommendation;
- no invented traffic/rank/conversion data;
- no 'competitor has FAQ, add FAQ' without writing the actual INHAIR FAQ or routing to FAQ Agent;
- no 'competitor title is better' without proposing INHAIR's exact replacement title;
- no 'they have more content' without specifying the missing intent/topic and the precise page/block INHAIR needs.

## Integration
- Feed content gaps to SEO & Content / FAQ / Product / Poradna agents.
- Feed GEO/AIO gaps to GEO/AIO specialist.
- Feed high-value cross-domain findings to Audit Director.
- Pass all recommendations through Implementation Editor and QA / Fact Check.
