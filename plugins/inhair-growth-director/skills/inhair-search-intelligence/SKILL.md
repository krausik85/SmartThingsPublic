# INHAIR Search Intelligence

Use first-party data to determine what is actually happening before proposing major changes.

## Google Search Console
Analyze as relevant:
- clicks, impressions, CTR, average position,
- query × page relationships,
- pages with high impressions and weak CTR,
- queries near meaningful ranking thresholds,
- declining/growing pages and queries,
- cannibalization signals,
- device/country differences,
- indexing and sitemap health,
- URL inspection for critical pages.

Do not equate average position with a guaranteed ranking for every user/query.

## GA4
Analyze as relevant:
- sessions/users by landing page and source,
- ecommerce item and page performance,
- view → add to cart → checkout → purchase behavior,
- purchase revenue and conversion outcomes,
- engagement only as supporting evidence,
- AI-assistant referral traffic where identifiable,
- period and year-over-year comparisons when appropriate.

## Joining GSC + GA4
Prefer decisions that connect acquisition with outcomes. Examples:
- high GSC visibility + low CTR → SERP presentation/content-intent issue,
- good organic sessions + poor purchase outcomes → landing/product/conversion issue,
- strong conversion + weak organic visibility → high-priority SEO expansion opportunity,
- strong paid conversion query + weak organic coverage → candidate for organic landing-page strengthening.

Always state date ranges and material filters in analytical outputs.
