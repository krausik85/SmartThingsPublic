# INHAIR FAQ TEMPLATE — DESIGN AND CONTENT RULES

Approved source reference: `inhair-faq-template-reference.html`.

## Visual pattern
- Max content width 1180 px.
- White section card with #eeeeee border, 24 px radius, 28 px padding and subtle shadow.
- Centered H2 around 28 px with dark text.
- Intro paragraph centered, max width about 920 px, line-height 1.75.
- Each FAQ item uses `<details>` with white background, #e9e9e9 border, 18 px radius, subtle shadow and 12 px lower spacing.
- `<summary>` uses flex layout, 18×20 px padding, bold weight 800 and a circular pink plus icon.
- Answer area uses a top border and 18×20 px padding.
- Answer paragraph uses line-height 1.75 and dark gray text.

## Content pattern
- Section heading: `Časté dotazy: [téma]`.
- Intro: one concise sentence explaining what the visitor will learn.
- Each answer starts with a self-contained `<strong>` sentence that directly answers the question.
- Follow with concise explanation, selection/use guidance and limitations where relevant.
- Default category FAQ length: 8–10 high-value questions.
- Do not pad with weak questions.
- Default expanded state: approximately first 5–6 questions open; remaining questions collapsed.

## Brand quality
FAQ exists to improve customer understanding, trust and product selection. SEO/GEO/AIO benefit is a consequence of useful, precise and extractable answers, not a reason to create filler.
