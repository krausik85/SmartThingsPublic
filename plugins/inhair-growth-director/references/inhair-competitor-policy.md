# INHAIR competitor benchmark policy

Primary competitor set supplied by the owner:

1. **NádhernéVlasy.cz** — https://www.nadhernevlasy.cz/
   - primary general e-commerce competitor / benchmark for the overall INHAIR shop;
   - compare assortment presentation, category architecture, product-page merchandising, promotions, content depth, internal linking, search intent coverage, pricing/offer framing when current data is available, trust signals and conversion paths.

2. **ItalyStyle.cz** — https://www.italystyle.cz/
   - high-priority benchmark especially for **Framesi** and adjacent professional hairdressing assortment/content;
   - use more heavily for Framesi brand/category/product audits, professional positioning, brand education and merchandising.

3. **NaVlas.cz** — https://www.navlas.cz/
   - secondary general benchmark for professional hair cosmetics, category/search coverage, product presentation and content architecture.

## Rules
- Competitor pages are dynamic public sources: always verify current live content before making specific claims.
- Do not copy competitor wording, structure or proprietary creative. Extract patterns, gaps, strengths and opportunities, then create original INHAIR implementations.
- Never assume a competitor ranks better, converts better, has lower prices or stronger authority without current evidence.
- Separate observed fact from inference.
- Competitor evidence can help prioritize but does not override first-party INHAIR data.
- When competitor evidence reveals an opportunity, the final output must say exactly what INHAIR should change, where, and with ready-to-use replacement/action.
- If a competitor feature would weaken INHAIR's core positioning of expertise + trust + correct product choice, do not imitate it merely because a competitor uses it.

## Default benchmark dimensions
- SERP/search intent coverage and landing-page type;
- title/meta/H1/snippet positioning;
- category taxonomy and faceted navigation;
- brand and product-line architecture;
- product-page information quality, parameters, INCI, usage, media, trust and cross-sell;
- FAQ/advice/blog/academy depth and originality;
- internal linking and customer journey from problem -> category -> product;
- GEO/AIO answerability, factual clarity, entity signals and sourceability;
- commercial offer presentation, promotions, bundles and delivery/trust signals;
- mobile usability and conversion friction when observable;
- structured data / technical signals when accessible.

## Framesi rule
For Framesi-specific tasks, use **ItalyStyle.cz** as the first external benchmark, then NádhernéVlasy.cz and NaVlas.cz where relevant. For general INHAIR audits, use NádhernéVlasy.cz first.
