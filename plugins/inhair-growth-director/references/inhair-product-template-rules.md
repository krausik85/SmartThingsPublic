# INHAIR PRODUCT TEMPLATE — CONTENT AND HTML RULES

Approved exemplar: `inhair-product-template-example.html`.

## Purpose
Create expert, commercially useful product descriptions for INHAIR.cz that combine readable sales copy with verifiable technical detail. Preserve the structure and HTML style of the approved exemplar, while changing all facts to the specific product.

## Required order
1. Opening `<p style="text-align: justify;">` with **full Czech product name**, original/international name in italics when relevant, product type, who it is for, primary problem, verified mechanism/technology and realistic result.
2. 5–8 benefit lines in `<p style="padding-left: 40px;"><strong>✓ ...</strong></p>` format.
3. `<hr>` + `<blockquote>` explaining the product line/range only when verified and useful.
4. `<hr>`.
5. `Doporučeno pro:` paragraph.
6. `Ochrana proti:` or a more accurate equivalent when “protection” is not factually appropriate.
7. `Obsahuje:` active-ingredient/technology paragraphs, each indented 40 px, with Czech name + INCI/technical name in italics where verified.
8. `Použití:` exact verified directions.
9. `Složení:` exact verified INCI, preserving the supplied order and spelling.

## Evidence rules
- Never invent ingredients, percentages, technologies, test results, timing, temperatures, hold times, vegan status, sulfate/silicone claims or effects.
- If INCI is supplied, interpret ingredients conservatively; do not infer clinical efficacy from presence alone.
- Manufacturer claims must not be strengthened.
- Product-line block is optional if the line information is not verified.
- Avoid medical claims.
- If product instructions conflict across sources, flag the conflict rather than choosing silently.

## Style
- Natural polished Czech, expert but readable.
- Strong commercial hook in the first paragraph without hype.
- Use bold for genuinely important terms, not keyword stuffing.
- Avoid generic AI filler and repetitive claims.
- Prefer concrete hair/scalp situations and selection guidance.
- Preserve HTML-only output when the user requests copy for the shop.
