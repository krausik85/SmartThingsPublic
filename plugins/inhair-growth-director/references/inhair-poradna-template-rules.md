# INHAIR PORADNA TEMPLATE — STRUCTURE AND CONTENT RULES

Approved full exemplar: `inhair-poradna-template.html`.

## Purpose
Create long-form expert advice pages that solve a real customer problem, demonstrate INHAIR expertise, support SEO/GEO/AIO and lead naturally to appropriate products without turning the article into a catalogue.

## Core structure
Use the approved exemplar as the visual source of truth. Adapt sections to the topic, but preserve its logic:
1. Hero / article introduction with INHAIR expert-advice label, H1 and a strong factual opening.
2. `Dotaz z poradny` customer/model situation.
3. `Rychlá odpověď experta` — immediate, useful answer before deep explanation.
4. Expert explanation / diagnosis of mechanisms and distinctions.
5. Diagnostic decision tree / segmentation according to the problem.
6. Practical routine / step-by-step plan.
7. Recommended product care with verified INHAIR product URLs and only relevant products.
8. Optional specialist/booster section when justified.
9. Practical application/use tips.
10. Common mistakes and safety/limitations.
11. FAQ with concise direct answers.
12. Final CTA to advice/category/product/diagnostic tool as appropriate.
13. Structured data only when semantically correct and matching visible content.

## Expert standard
- Direct answer first, then depth.
- Distinguish hair fibre from scalp/follicle issues.
- State cosmetic limits and referral/red flags where relevant.
- Verify scientific/technical claims and product facts.
- Do not manufacture “diagnostic tests” or causal mechanisms that are not reliable.
- Product blocks must contain current direct INHAIR.cz URLs.
- Do not use fake urgency, fake clinical certainty or exaggerated recovery timelines.

## Commercial standard
The article must create a natural path: problem -> understanding -> routine -> appropriate category/product -> optional further advice. Product recommendations must be justified by the described need.
